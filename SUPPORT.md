# Support

Thanks for using Hartwell Labs software. Here is how to get help.

## Documentation first

Most "how do I…" questions are answered by the project's own `README.md`, its
`docs/` directory, and the [docs site](https://bartoszosiej.github.io/Docs/).

## Questions and ideas

- Open a **Discussion** in the relevant repository (or in the
  [profile discussions](https://github.com/BartoszOsiej/BartoszOsiej/discussions)).
- For bugs, open an **issue** using the bug report template — include your OS,
  version, and exact steps to reproduce.

## Business and partnerships

- **contact@hartwell-labs.pl**
- **business@hartwell-labs.pl** — commercial licensing, integrations, audits.
- Website: [hartwell-labs.pl](https://hartwell-labs.pl)

## Security

Do **not** use issues or discussions for vulnerabilities. See
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

## What we cannot help with

- General debugging of your own application unrelated to our software.
- Windows/macOS support beyond what a project explicitly lists (most products
  are Linux-first).

Response times are best-effort — this is an independent lab, not a help desk.
