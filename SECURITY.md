# Security Policy

Hartwell Labs builds security software. We take the security of our own code as
seriously as we expect our users to take theirs.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are applied to the latest release of each product. Where a
project publishes a versioned series, the most recent minor release is
supported; older releases are best-effort.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report privately to one of:

- **bartosz.osiej2007@gmail.com** — security reports / coordinated disclosure
- **contact@hartwell-labs.pl** — general & business

Please include:

- the affected project and version / commit,
- a description of the issue and its impact,
- a proof of concept or reproduction steps, if available,
- any suggested remediation.

You will receive an acknowledgement **within 48 hours**. We will keep you
updated as we triage, and we will credit reporters in the affected product's
Hall of Fame (unless you ask us not to).

## Coordinated disclosure

We ask that you give us a reasonable window to ship a fix before publishing.
We aim to fix confirmed critical issues within 30 days.

## Scope

In scope: repositories under this organization that we maintain.

Out of scope: third-party dependencies (report those upstream — though we're
happy to help coordinate), social engineering, and denial-of-service against
our infrastructure.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith,
avoid privacy violations and service disruption, and only interact with
accounts and data they own or have permission to test.
