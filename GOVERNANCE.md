# Governance

Hartwell Labs is an independent engineering lab. This document describes how
decisions are made across the organization and its open-source projects.

## Model

Hartwell Labs operates as a **benevolent-dictator / maintainer** model. The
founder holds final say on project direction, releases, and licensing. Day-to-day
contributions are governed by [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Principles

1. **Code over claims.** A feature exists when it ships with tests and CI.
2. **Own your stack.** Zero dependencies where possible, auditable everywhere.
3. **Open by default.** MIT licensed; issues and pull requests are welcome.
4. **Visibility is defence.** You cannot protect what you cannot see.

## Roles

- **Founder / Lead maintainer** — final decision on scope, releases, security.
- **Maintainers** — invited per repository; can review and merge.
- **Contributors** — anyone who submits an accepted pull request.

## Changes to governance

Changes to this document are made by the founder and announced in the org's
[profile discussions](https://github.com/BartoszOsiej/BartoszOsiej/discussions).
